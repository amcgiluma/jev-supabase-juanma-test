SECURITY OPERATIONS / DECISION DESIGN

A practical triage playbook.

Keep the model’s job narrow. Keep access decisions with your security team.

01 / CHOICE

Find the right workstream

Classify the request into identity and access, security incident, data and privacy, platform security, or other. The extra “other” path keeps uncertain tickets visible.

02 / SCORE

Prioritize the queue

Rate urgency against a four level rubric, from routine to active compromise. The score is probability weighted; use the distribution when reviewing borderline cases.

03 / NOUL

Flag human review

Estimate whether a person should review before any account or security action. For MFA resets, identity verification and policy checks remain mandatory.

✳

Suggested workflow

Jev returns signals → your code applies policy and thresholds → an analyst verifies identity and makes the final decision. Start with labeled historical tickets before automating even low risk routing.
